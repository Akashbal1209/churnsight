# Seller Communication and Escalation (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

## Seller response time
Sellers must answer customer messages within **2 business days**.

## Escalation
If a seller does not answer within 2 business days, or the customer reports that requests for a return, exchange or cancellation were ignored, marketplace support takes over the case and contacts the customer directly within **1 business day**.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before shipping. If a cancellation was requested but the item was still delivered, follow *Returns and Refunds* (right of withdrawal) at no cost to the customer.

## Seller accountability
Repeated complaints about the same seller (late shipping, wrong items, no response) are recorded on the seller's quality score and reviewed monthly by the Seller Quality team.
