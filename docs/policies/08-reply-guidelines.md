# Customer Reply Guidelines (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

## Language and tone
- Reply in **Brazilian Portuguese**, in a warm and direct tone.
- Start with a short, specific apology that names the problem the customer described.
- Never blame the customer, the carrier or the seller in the reply.

## Content
- State the concrete next step and its deadline (for example, "abriremos uma investigação em até 2 dias úteis").
- Offer only resolutions and vouchers that the policies allow, with their exact amounts and conditions.
- If the case needs information we do not have (photos, order number, address), ask for it explicitly.
- If the situation is not covered by any policy, or the customer mentions legal action, consumer agencies (Procon) or a health/safety risk, **escalate to a human agent** instead of promising anything.

## Format
Three to six sentences. Sign as "Equipe Aurora".
