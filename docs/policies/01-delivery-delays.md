# Delivery Delays Policy (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

## Delivery estimates
Every order shows an estimated delivery date at checkout. The estimate already includes the seller's handling time and the carrier's transit time.

## When an order is late
- **Up to 7 days past the estimate:** apologise, share the current tracking status, and confirm a new expected date with the carrier.
- **More than 7 days past the estimate:** in addition, the customer may receive a **R$20 delay voucher** (code issued by support, valid 60 days, minimum next purchase R$60).
- **More than 15 days past the estimate, or 30 days after purchase with no delivery:** the customer may choose a **full refund** instead of waiting. See *Order Not Received*.

## What support must not do
- Do not promise a specific delivery date unless the carrier has confirmed it.
- Do not blame the carrier publicly; the marketplace is responsible for the customer experience.
