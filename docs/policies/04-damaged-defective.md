# Damaged or Defective Items Policy (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

## Reporting
Customers should report damage or a defect with **photos** through the order page. Damage visible on arrival (broken item, leaking bottle, crushed box with damaged contents) should be reported within **7 days** of delivery when possible; defects found later follow the CDC deadlines in *Returns and Refunds*.

## Resolution
- The customer chooses a **free replacement** or a **full refund**.
- For items under **R$50**, the customer does not need to send the item back; the refund or replacement is processed after the photos are reviewed.
- Suspected counterfeit or non-original items are escalated to the Seller Quality team and the listing is reviewed.

## Packaging-only damage
If only the outer box is damaged and the product works, apologise and record the case against the seller's packaging score. No compensation is required, but a goodwill voucher may be offered under *Retention and Goodwill Offers*.
