# Retention and Goodwill Offers (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

Goodwill offers exist to win back customers after a bad first experience. They are limited and must follow these rules.

## Allowed offers
- **R$15 goodwill voucher:** first-order customers who left a 1-2 star review.
- **R$30 goodwill voucher:** customers whose order value was **R$300 or more** and who had a negative experience.
- **R$20 delay voucher:** only for delays of more than 7 days (see *Delivery Delays*). Do not combine it with a goodwill voucher; offer the higher one.

## Rules
- Vouchers are valid for **60 days** with a **minimum next purchase of R$60**.
- **One goodwill voucher per customer every 6 months.**
- Never offer cash, discounts on the current order, or amounts other than those listed here.
- Vouchers complement the resolution (refund, replacement); they never replace it.
- Customers who are already being refunded for fraud or abuse are not eligible.
