# Order Not Received Policy (Aurora Marketplace)

*Synthetic knowledge base for a fictional marketplace, written for the ChurnSight demo.*

## Opening an investigation
If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or the order is past its estimated date and not delivered, support opens a carrier investigation within **2 business days**.

## Resolution
- If the parcel is confirmed lost or stolen, the customer chooses between **reshipping the same item at no cost** or a **full refund** (product + shipping).
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **10 business days** of the decision. Credit card refunds on installment purchases appear as credits on the next card statements.
- If the order is more than 30 days old with no delivery, the customer may request a full refund immediately, without waiting for the investigation.

## Communication
Support updates the customer at least every 3 business days until the case is closed.
